Dig

Marks cells for the Blueprints mod to dig out when the blueprint is pasted in the game.

{digCount, plural, =1 {1 cell} other {{{ digCount }} cells}} marked for digging

Click or drag on the blueprint to mark cells. A cell under a planning note is already dug in the game, so it cannot take a dig of its own (the cursor turns red). Right-click, or tap Select in the toolbar, to return to Select.
